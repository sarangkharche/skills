## Linked files and standard

Both downloadable files found in the navigation were reviewed: the [AI white paper](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/WhitePaper-ASD-STE100_and_AI.pdf), June 2026, three pages, and the [change form](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/CHANGE-FORM.docx). The form did not add writing guidance.

The About page links to the [official Issue 9 Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23666005), which resolved to record 23666006. The complete standard PDF was obtained from that record and contains 434 pages. Review covered the rule summaries for all nine writing sections, the copyright and usage notices, the dictionary introduction and selected explanations and entries. It was not an entry by entry audit of the entire dictionary or a full compliance assessment.

The site sections covered in the standard review were words, noun groups, verbs, sentences, procedures, descriptions, safety instructions, punctuation and word counting, and writing practices. The main additions to the skill were conditions before actions, the distinction between procedures and descriptions, clearer noun phrases and optional sentence length targets.

## Medium inspiration

The [original Medium article by Nikhil](https://medium.com/neuralnotions/andrej-karpathys-new-suggestion-ask-ai-output-in-asd-ste100-format-and-not-in-text-format-b7b753f52f32), published 3 October 2026, was reviewed in full from text supplied by the user on 11 November 2026. Reader comments were not used. Its practical writing advice reinforces direct sentence structure, consistent terminology, one action per instruction and removing filler. It recommends the spirit of the standard rather than presenting a complete implementation. The article says under twenty words for procedural sentences; the official rule permits up to 20. The skill uses the official limit as an optional target.

The full article also advocates diagrams, interactive HTML and animations as explanatory artifacts. Those ideas are documented here as context, not added as an automatic artifact workflow: this skill remains focused on technical writing. Claims about comprehension speed and software generation cost are the author's impressions and are not represented as measured benefits of this skill. Karpathy attribution remains the article's attribution; the original post was not directly verified.

## Boundaries

External social profiles, university courses and unrelated linked sites were not crawled. Request and change forms were not submitted. No contact messages were sent.

The skill contains original guidance and examples, not a copy of the website, full standard, dictionary or third party branding. The official record identifies a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license and the PDF includes its own copyright and special usage provisions. No additional redistribution rights are asserted here. This package is an independent practical writing aid with intentional adaptations, not an endorsed tool or a full implementation of ASD-STE100.
